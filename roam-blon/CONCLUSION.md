The system was successfully engineered to fulfill its objective of creating a web-based platform for tourism in the Municipality of Romblon, aimed at providing tourists with a convenient and personalized way to explore destinations, plan itineraries, and access local events. Additionally, user account registration and data from the gathered information were securely recorded in the database, allowing the administrator to efficiently verify users, manage tour-guide accounts, monitor tourist booking logs, and update or delete destinations and events.

The target features for the planned system were successfully applied such as: (1) A user-friendly interface for tourists, tour guides, and administrators; (2) Personalized AI-powered activity planning for tailored travel itineraries powered by Gemini 2.5; (3) Real-time updates on local events and attractions via Supabase Realtime; (4) Secure account verification for both tourists and tour guides through Supabase Auth with role-based access control; (5) Feedback and rating system for tourists to evaluate destinations and tour-guide experiences; (6) Administrative controls for managing users, tour-guides, destinations, events, and bookings efficiently; (7) Tour-guide booking system for scheduling and reserving guided tours with QR code confirmation; (8) Language translation feature to support both local and foreign tourists via AI chat; (9) AI-powered maps for optimized navigation and route planning within the municipality using Leaflet and OpenStreetMap.

The web-based platform was deemed acceptable and functional by tourists, tour guides, and the tourism office in the Municipality of Romblon and evaluated using the ISO/IEC 25010:2011 standard across 12 quality sub-characteristics. This confirmed that the Roam-Blón platform is ready for deployment, offering a reliable and quality-assured solution to enhance tourism in the Marble Capital of the Philippines.

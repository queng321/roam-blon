# Recommendations for Future Researchers

Future researchers who wish to adapt or enhance this study should expand the platform's local directory by adding restaurants, local delicacies, pharmacies, clinics, and hospitals to give tourists a complete picture of essential services. Integrating emergency hotlines and a real-time chat system between tourists and administrators will improve on-the-ground assistance and communication. For more accurate system evaluation, conduct user testing and surveys during the peak tourist season (March to May) when actual usage is highest. Additionally, deploying QR codes in public areas — tourist spots, transport terminals, and commercial establishments — will allow visitors to instantly access the platform and retrieve relevant information with a single scan.
